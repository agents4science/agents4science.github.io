# Call for Client Projects --- Winter 2027

*[AI Agents for Science](index.md) (CMSC 35370) is seeking 4--6 research groups to serve as project clients this winter.*

## The deal

In the final three weeks of the quarter (mid-February to mid-March 2027), teams of three students build a working AI agent for a real research group---yours---with instructor and TA support and course-provided compute. You get a working prototype, its complete execution traces, and a report; the students get a real problem with a real stakeholder.

Good client tasks are concrete, scoped to three weeks, and checkable. Examples of the right shape: Synthesize and organize the literature for a review in progress, with verified citations; triage and classify a backlog of instrument logs or pipeline failures; build an agent that monitors a data stream and drafts a weekly status report; automate a recurring analysis your group does by hand. If you are unsure whether a task fits, ask---scoping help is part of the offer.

## What we ask of clients

1. **A one-page task specification** --- the goal, what success looks like, and what is out of scope.
2. **Working data and code access before the sprint starts** --- accounts created, data shareable, licenses checked. This is the most common point of failure, so we ask for it up front.
3. **A named contact** willing to spend about 30 minutes per week with the team during the three-week sprint.
4. **Demo day attendance** (mid-March, date TBD) --- you come see what they built.

Teams whose client project falls through revert to the course's default project, so there is no penalty to students if circumstances change---but the four commitments above are what make a client project gradeable, and we hold to them.

## How to propose

Send the one-page specification to Ian Foster (foster@anl.gov) by **December 15, 2026**. We will confirm selected projects by early January.
